Old Computer Challenge 2025

2025-07-13

Although this post is dated 2025-07-13, the date when the 2025 edition of the Old Computer Challenge took place, this post was actually written in 2026-07-04, the day before the 2026 edition of the challenge is set to begin.

The reason for this date discrepancy is that, during OCC 2025 I decided to log all my retro adventures on my gopherhole, so there was no other way to get to this content via HTTP. I was certain I had at least published a post about this here, but apparently I forgot, so before the next edition of the challenge takes place, I decided to make this post so other participants that are not on Gopher can at least know where to get to my adventures from the previous year.

Although I'm not going to republish any of the content from that edition here, you can either use this link if you have a Gopher client, or if you don't and are unwilling to install one, you can use this link to access the content via a proxy.
